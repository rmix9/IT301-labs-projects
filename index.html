My Favorite NFL Team

My favorite NFL team is the Baltimore Ravens. The Ravens are a professional football team based in Baltimore, Maryland. They are a member of the AFC North division in the NFL. The team was established in 1996 and has since become one of the most successful franchises in the league.

The Ravens have won two Super Bowl championships, in 2000 and 2012, and have made numerous playoff appearances. They are known for their strong defense, led by legendary players such as Ray Lewis and Ed Reed. The team's colors are purple, black, and gold, and their mascot is named "Poe," after the famous poet Edgar Allan Poe.
